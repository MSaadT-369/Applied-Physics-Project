About Refraction

Refraction occurs when light passes from one medium to another, changing speed and direction. The amount of bending depends on the angle of incidence and the refractive indices of the materials.

Snell's Law

\( n_1 \sin \theta_1 = n_2 \sin \theta_2 \)

Where:

  • \( n_1, n_2 \): Refractive indices of the two media
  • \( \theta_1 \): Angle of incidence
  • \( \theta_2 \): Angle of refraction
